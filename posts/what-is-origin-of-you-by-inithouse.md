# What is Origin Of You by Inithouse?

Origin Of You is a self-discovery app that combines five systems and 120+ data points into an AI-generated written portrait of you.

Not a quiz result. Not a personality type. A written portrait, several pages long, grounded in calculations you can trace back to your birth data. You enter your date, time, and place of birth. The app runs the math across five different systems. An AI drafts a portrait from those calculations. The result reads like a letter written to you specifically, not a category you share with a few million other people.

## Category

Self-discovery app. Sits between personality quizzes (16Personalities, MBTI tests) and astrology apps that push daily horoscopes. Uses elements from both worlds but produces something neither does: a continuous written text about you, grounded in over a hundred computed data points.

## The five systems

Origin Of You reads five systems together, not in isolation:

| System | What it contributes | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Astrology | Sun, Moon, Rising, planetary positions | Date, time, place of birth |
| Numerology | Life path, expression, soul urge numbers | Full birth name + birth date |
| Tarot Birth Cards | Archetypal pair linked to birth date | Birth date calculation |
| Chinese zodiac | Year animal + element | Birth year |
| Lunar phase | Moon phase at the moment of birth | Date of birth |

Each system adds a different lens. The portrait weaves them into one text, contradictions included. If your numerology says you crave stability but your Moon sign says you run from it, the portrait says both.

## How it works

1. Enter your birth date, time, and place. Takes about 90 seconds, no account required.
2. The app runs 120+ calculations across all five systems.
3. An AI drafts a portrait from those calculations.
4. The result is a written text about you, not a list of traits or a four-letter code.

Math first, prose second. Every sentence in the portrait traces back to a specific calculation. The AI writes, but the framework constrains what it can say. No flattery, no scare tactics, no vague statements that could apply to anyone.

## Who uses it

People who find personality quizzes too reductive. People curious about astrology but tired of daily horoscope notifications. Writers and creators looking for a different mirror. Therapists' clients between sessions. Anyone who wants to see themselves through multiple lenses at once rather than a single framework.

## What it is not

Origin Of You is not a fortune-telling app. There are no predictions, no "what will happen next week," no compatibility readings. The portrait describes patterns in who you are based on your birth data. It does not claim to know your future.

It is not a quiz. There are no questions to answer, no right or wrong responses. The only input is your birth data.

It is not an astrology app in the horoscope sense. There is no daily notification, no weekly forecast, no retrograde alerts.

## Build notes

We built Origin Of You because existing self-discovery tools force a choice: either a 10-minute quiz that gives you a type code, or an astrology app that sends you a notification every morning. Both reduce a person to a category.

The experiment: what if we combined multiple systems, ran the math, and let an AI write a portrait constrained by those calculations? The result would be specific (grounded in 120+ data points) and readable (prose, not a spreadsheet of planetary positions).

The five systems were chosen because they approach the same person from different angles. Astrology works with planetary positions. Numerology works with names and numbers. Tarot Birth Cards work with archetypes. The Chinese zodiac works with cycles. Lunar phases work with timing. Where they disagree about you is often where the portrait gets interesting.

The constraint matters. Without it, an AI writing about "who you are" drifts into flattery or generic wisdom. Pinning every claim to a calculation keeps the text honest. You can check the math yourself.
